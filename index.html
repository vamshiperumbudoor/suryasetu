01 · PROBLEM DEFINITION

Why current solar options do not fully solve this challenge

The barrier is not simply installing photovoltaic panels. Farmers need dependable water from deep, variable borewells, at a price that competes with effectively free grid electricity.

01

High upfront cost

Conventional solar pump packages can be unaffordable. A new pump is also unnecessary where a serviceable motor can be retained.

02

Deep, hard-rock borewells

Depth alone does not size a pump. Dynamic water level, total dynamic head, discharge requirement, pipe losses and seasonal drawdown matter.

03

Daylight-only operation

Solar-only pumping can fail to meet a farmer's irrigation window during cloudy periods or outside solar hours.

04

Theft and tampering

Panels, copper cable, controller enclosures and meter wiring need physical security, asset identity and tamper evidence.

05

Unverified energy accounting

Grid backup must be measured independently; solar generation, grid import and any approved export must not be conflated.

06

Groundwater + service risk

Reliable pumping must not encourage over-extraction. Remote sites also need local technicians, spare parts and fast fault response.

02 · PROPOSED SOLUTION

SuryaSetu: a modular solar-first hybrid retrofit kit

One common control and monitoring platform, configured around the borewell and existing motor. Replace only what the site audit proves is necessary.

PATH A · RETROFIT FIRST

Keep a compatible existing motor

For a healthy 3–10 HP AC motor: add a correctly rated solar pump drive / hybrid VFD, PV array, protected grid input, changeover logic, metering and safety sensors. Retain the pump and rising main after inspection and performance testing.

  • Lower material replacement and installation cost
  • Less disruption to the farmer's existing borewell
  • Motor compatibility, insulation and pump-curve checks before approval
PATH B · NEW SYSTEM

Supply an integrated solar-hybrid pump

Where the old motor or pump is damaged, inefficient or mismatched to the borewell: supply a matched 3–10 HP pump, motor, solar drive, PV modules, grid backup interface and protection package.

  • Matched pump curve for measured site duty point
  • Standardised modular 3 / 5 / 7.5 / 10 HP product family
  • Common controller, meter, spares and service procedures
Design rule: do not size the pump from borewell depth or HP label alone. A qualified site survey must measure static and pumping water levels, target flow, total dynamic head, pipe diameter/length, water quality and motor electrical characteristics.
03 · ENGINEERING DESIGN

How the complete system works

Solar supplies the pump first. Grid backup is allowed only through a protected, separately metered path. The controller enforces pump and groundwater limits locally, even when mobile connectivity is unavailable.

01 · GENERATEPV array

Modular strings, string protection, earthing and surge protection.

02 · CONVERTHybrid solar drive

MPPT, motor-compatible VFD, controlled grid input and safe changeover.

03 · PUMP3–10 HP motor + pump

Selected for measured head and flow, with dry-run and overload interlocks.

04 · MEASUREEnergy + water sensors

Separate grid import/export registers, PV data, flow and borewell level.

05 · VERIFYSecure controller + app

Signed events, GPS asset register, alerts, offline buffer and audit trail.

Solar-first dispatch

MPPT drive runs the motor from available PV within the pump's safe operating envelope. If solar is insufficient, the controller checks farmer schedule, water limits and grid permission before transferring to grid input.

Metering that can be audited

Use a utility-approved bidirectional meter where export is permitted, with separate import/export registers. Add a sealed sub-meter or certified measurement channel for controller-level reconciliation; sign readings and log enclosure-open events.

Deep-borewell protection

Use dry-run / underload detection, low-water-level cut-out, flow verification, over/under-voltage, phase loss, overcurrent, overload, over-temperature and controlled restart delay.

04 · SECURITY + WATER STEWARDSHIP

Make theft, tampering and over-pumping harder to hide

Physical anti-theft package

  • Anti-theft module fasteners and concealed, tamper-resistant cable conduits
  • Reinforced PV mounting with anti-lift clamps and foundation anchors
  • Locked, ventilated inverter/controller cabinet with enclosure switch
  • Serial-number register for every panel, inverter, drive and meter
  • GPS-tagged commissioning photos, geofence and movement alert
  • Optional cable continuity / cabinet vibration sensor with local siren

Tamper-evident energy chain

  • Utility meter remains the settlement authority for grid energy
  • Meter seals, terminal-cover switch and reverse-current / bypass anomaly checks
  • Signed, time-stamped meter snapshots stored locally during network outages
  • Reconcile meter import, inverter input and controller runtime daily
  • Role-based corrections; immutable audit record for every adjustment
  • Export only after DISCOM interconnection approval and anti-islanding protection

Groundwater accounting

  • Inline flow meter records litres / kilolitres pumped
  • Water-level sensor tracks static and pumping level trends
  • Set site-specific daily / seasonal pumping limits with farmer and local authority
  • Stop or alert on low water level, abnormal drawdown or flow mismatch
  • Dashboard flags rising water use per acre or falling yield per kWh

Service that reaches the village

  • District service partners and trained local technicians
  • Common spare kit: fuses, SPD, sensors, contactors, fans and drive modules
  • Remote diagnostics plus offline technician checklist
  • Ticket severity, dispatch tracking, warranty and preventive maintenance
  • Proposed service target: acknowledge within 2 hours; on-site target within 24–48 hours, subject to district geography and contract

06 · SITE SIZING + FARMER VALUE

Size for water delivered, not panel capacity alone

For each borewell, measure the duty point first. The same 5 HP label can deliver very different water volumes at different total dynamic heads.

Minimum site survey

  1. Record borewell depth, casing, static water level and pumping water level.
  2. Conduct a controlled pumping test to estimate sustainable yield and drawdown.
  3. Measure total dynamic head: lift + delivery pressure + friction losses.
  4. Specify target discharge (m³/hour) and irrigation hours / crop-water requirement.
  5. Inspect motor nameplate, phase, voltage, current, insulation and pump condition.
  6. Size PV and drive using pump curves, solar resource, seasonal duty and electrical losses.

Farmer-facing value proposition

  • Keep the existing motor where technically sound.
  • Use solar energy when available; grid fallback avoids a daylight-only trap.
  • Show solar generation, grid units imported and water pumped separately.
  • Warn before dry-run, low-water, voltage or tamper faults become failures.
  • Track service tickets and warranty in Telugu and English.
  • Show the farmer the verified total cost, financing and any subsidy terms before consent.
Hydraulic sizing reference: hydraulic power is proportional to water density × gravity × flow × total dynamic head. Required motor input is higher after pump, motor and drive losses. Final HP and PV capacity must be selected from measured site data and manufacturer pump curves by a qualified engineer.
07 · IMPLEMENTATION PLAN

Build, test and prove it in Telangana before scaling

A staged pilot makes the 30% cost target, borewell performance, theft resistance and actual subsidy impact measurable.

0–8 weeks · Validate

Survey 30–50 representative borewells; audit existing motors; map head, flow, power quality, theft risk, vendors and DISCOM rules.

2–4 months · Prototype

Build 3, 5, 7.5 and 10 HP variants; bench-test hybrid drive, dry-run logic, metering, tamper events and fail-safe changeover.

4–9 months · Field pilot

Install 100–300 systems across varied districts and geology; compare retrofit and new-pump pathways with baseline sites.

9–18 months · Scale gate

Independent cost, uptime, energy, water, theft and farmer-satisfaction evaluation; approve procurement and district service model.

The ₹14,000 crore figure is the challenge brief's stated annual free-power burden. It is not a promise that the same amount can be saved. Actual savings depend on adoption, solar output, residual grid imports, metering and the state's settlement rules.

Groundwater protection

09 · DESIGN CLARIFICATIONS

Key questions the solution must answer

Does SuryaSetu export excess solar power to the grid?

Only where the relevant DISCOM permits it and the installation has approved interconnection, a compliant bidirectional meter, protection and settlement agreement. The default design does not assume export revenue.

Can the kit work with every existing 3–10 HP motor?

No. Each motor must pass compatibility checks for phase, voltage, current, insulation, starting characteristics and pump condition. Incompatible or unsafe assets follow the new-system path.

How is the 30% cheaper target achieved?

Through retrofit-first eligibility, standardised modular components, bulk procurement, repeatable installation and local service. It must be proven against a like-for-like baseline with equivalent performance, protection and warranty.

Does this website control a real pump?

No. This is a solution-design and demonstration website. Live control, meter readings, GPS events, farmer accounts and service tickets require certified field hardware, a secure backend, authenticated devices and operational integrations.
