PRODUCT BLUEPRINT

How SuryaSetu works

An integrated field device, farmer app, service network and state platform.

Important: This GitHub Pages prototype is a functional front-end demonstration with simulated records and local calculations. It does not yet control physical pumps, read utility meters, authenticate field devices or process payments. Those require hardware commissioning, backend services, security review and regulatory approvals.

System architecture

01 · Field hardware

PV array, hybrid inverter, existing or new pump motor, flow meter, water-level sensor, voltage/current sensing, tamper switches, GPS/cellular controller.

02 · Edge controller

Safe local control, dry-run/overload interlocks, solar-first dispatch, grid changeover, offline event queue and signed device telemetry.

03 · Secure platform

Device identity, encrypted ingestion, time-series data, role-based access, alert rules, audit ledger and API integrations.

04 · Farmer experience

Telugu/English UI, pump status, energy/water readings, outage alerts, service requests and offline-friendly app.

05 · Service operations

Technician dispatch, spare parts, warranty, preventive maintenance, remote diagnostics and escalation SLA.

06 · State / DISCOM

Verified energy accounting, approved export settlement, subsidy reconciliation, district analytics and programme audit.

Key engineering risks to solve

1
Deep hard-rock borewell sizingDynamic head and drawdown vary widely; validate pump curves and motor compatibility at each site.
2
30% cost-down without unsafe compromisesStandardised kits, local assembly, right-sized PV, retrofit compatibility and lifecycle procurement.
3
Grid metering and export permissionUtility-approved bidirectional meter, protection, anti-islanding and settlement agreement.
4
Groundwater sustainabilityMeter discharge and water level; pumping rules require local hydrogeology and governance.

Delivery roadmap

01
0–8 weeks · Design validationField survey, motor audit, pump curves, BOM, safety and DISCOM consultations.
02
2–4 months · Prototype3/5/7.5/10 HP bench and field prototypes; metering and protection validation.
03
4–9 months · Pilot100–300 diverse borewells; measure uptime, cost, water, theft and service outcomes.
04
9–18 months · Scale gateIndependent evaluation, procurement framework, financing and district rollout.

Indicative integration checklist

Identity & access

Farmer, technician, vendor, DISCOM and admin roles; MFA for privileged actions.

Data & APIs

REST/HTTPS ingestion, MQTT device channel, time-series database and event audit.

Safety & compliance

Electrical protections, grid interconnection, privacy, consent, retention and security testing.
